What To Do Before You Arrive

Hold it by the crown, rinse gently and keep it in milk. Come to us within the hour.

Save every piece, avoid chewing on that side and bring the pieces with you.

Call us immediately. If breathing or swallowing is hard, call 911 first.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

A dental emergency includes severe tooth pain, swelling of the face or gums, a knocked out tooth, a cracked or broken tooth, uncontrolled bleeding, a lost crown or an abscess. If you are unsure, call us and we will help you decide quickly.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. Our Philadelphia office answers calls and treats urgent patients 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Can I get a same-day root canal?

In many cases yes. After an exam and imaging, we can often complete a root canal in a single visit. Severe infections may need a short healing period, and we will explain this honestly.

What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown, not the root, rinse it gently and try to place it back in the socket. If that is not possible, keep it in milk and come to our office within an hour.

How can I pay for treatment?

We accept cash and credit cards. Our team explains the cost of treatment before it begins so there are no surprises.

Do you treat patients who are afraid of the dentist?

Absolutely. We use gentle techniques, thorough numbing and clear communication, and we move at a pace that keeps you comfortable.
